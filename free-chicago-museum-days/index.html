Free Chicago Museum Days 2026

Museums across the Chicagoland area are offering free days of admission to Illinois residents!

January 5, 2026

Chicago is home to some of the most respected cultural institutions in the country, and throughout 2026, many of them are offering Chicago free museum days for Illinois residents. These special admission dates make it easier to explore the best Chicago museums without the standard ticket cost.

From art and history to science and marine life, free admission days provide valuable opportunities for families, students, and local residents looking for meaningful and affordable experiences. If you’re planning ahead, review the full 2026 schedule below and consider pairing your visit with convenient lakefront transportation from Shoreline Sightseeing.

Museum Campus Free Admission Days 2026

Located along Lake Michigan, Museum Campus is home to three of the most popular museums in the city, making it easy to combine multiple stops into one full day of sightseeing.

Adler Planetarium

The Adler Planetarium offers immersive sky shows, space exploration exhibits, and hands-on science experiences that make it one of the most engaging family-friendly things to do in Chicago.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • January 7, 12, 14, 21, 26, 28
  • February 2, 4, 9, 11, 18, 23
  • March 2, 4, 9, 11, 18, 25
  • April 1, 8, 15, 22, 29

Tickets must be reserved online in advance. Admission is always free for Illinois teachers, active-duty military personnel, Chicago Park District employees, and select additional groups.

Location: 1300 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive

Field Museum

Home to SUE the T. rex and world-class natural history exhibits, the Field Museum is consistently ranked among the best Chicago museums for science and culture.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • Every Wednesday throughout 2026

Free tickets are available for advance reservation online or on site. Admission includes all general admission exhibitions. Illinois teachers and active-duty military personnel receive free general admission year-round.

Location: 1400 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive

Shedd Aquarium

The Shedd Aquarium features marine life exhibits, aquatic habitats, and interactive displays, making it one of the most popular family-friendly things to do in Chicago.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • January 6-8, 13-20, 27-29
  • February 3-5, 10-12, 17-19, 24-26

Free Nights (5 p.m.-9 p.m.):

  • March 3, 10, 17, 24, 31
  • April 7, 14, 21, 28
  • May 5, 12, 19, 26
  • June 2, 9, 16, 23, 30

Free admission includes permanent exhibits. Admission is always free for Chicago police officers and firefighters, active-duty U.S. military personnel, and educators from Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, and Wisconsin.

Location: 1200 S. DuSable Lake Shore Drive

Additional Chicago Free Museum Days 2026

Art Institute of Chicago

The Art Institute houses one of the most renowned art collections in the country, featuring iconic works across centuries and styles.

Free Winter Weekdays (Illinois Residents, January-February):

  • January 5-February 28, 2026

Free Evening Hours (Illinois Residents):

  • Third Thursdays from 5 p.m.-8 p.m., beginning April 16, 2026
  • Summer Thursdays from 5 p.m.-8 p.m., June 11-September 17, 2026

Free admission runs from 11 a.m. through closing. Admission is always free for Chicago teens under 18, children under 14, Link and WIC cardholders, active-duty military personnel, and current Illinois teachers.

Griffin Museum of Science and Industry

The Griffin Museum of Science and Industry offers interactive science exhibits, hands-on learning environments, and immersive experiences for visitors of all ages.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • January 12, 13, 14, 25, 26, 27, 28
  • February 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 17, 18, 19, 23, 24, 25, 26
  • April 19
  • June 4
  • June 19

Admission is always free for active-duty U.S. military personnel and veterans, Illinois POWs, Chicago police officers and firefighters, and Illinois teachers.

Chicago History Museum

The Chicago History Museum explores the city’s development, culture, and defining historical moments through engaging exhibitions and archives.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • January 19-23 and 27-30
  • February 16
  • March 4, 12, 25
  • April 1, 22, 30
  • May 6, 25

Admission is always free for Illinois residents under 18, children under 12, Illinois teachers, active-duty military personnel and veterans, and Chicago police officers and firefighters.

DuSable Black History Museum

The DuSable Black History Museum and Education Center preserves and celebrates African American history, culture, and contributions through exhibitions and programming.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • Every Wednesday throughout 2026

Admission is always free for military personnel and first responders, University of Chicago students, Chicago Park District employees, Illinois public school students, and other qualifying groups.

Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago

The Museum of Contemporary Art showcases cutting-edge exhibitions, rotating contemporary works, and public programming focused on modern artistic expression.

Illinois Resident Free Admission:

  • Every Tuesday from 5 p.m.-9 p.m.

The MCA operates under a pay-what-you-can policy, and no one will be turned away. Admission is always free for visitors under 18, visitors with disabilities and caregivers, active members of the military, police, and fire departments, veterans and their families, and Illinois elementary and high school teachers.

Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum

The Peggy Notebaert Nature Museum focuses on environmental education, wildlife exhibits, and hands-on science experiences for children and families.

Illinois Resident Free Admission Dates:

  • March 9-11
  • June 1-3
  • June 22-24
  • July 20-22
  • August 24-26
  • September 8-11
  • September 14-18
  • September 21-25
  • September 28-October 2
  • October 5-9
  • November 2-4
  • November 30-December 2
  • December 7-8

Visitors may reserve free tickets online or in person.

Recurring Free Museum Days in Chicago

In addition to major institutions, several Chicago museums offer recurring free admission days throughout the year. These options provide additional opportunities for residents to explore cultural attractions on a regular basis.

  • American Writers Museum: Free admission on the third Sunday of every month
  • Glessner House: Free tours for Illinois residents on the first and third Thursday of each month
  • Swedish American Museum: Free admission every second Tuesday of the month
  • Lizzadro Museum of Lapidary Art: Free admission every Wednesday
  • Heritage Museum of Asian Art: Free admission on the third Friday evening of each month
  • Driehaus Museum: Free admission on Wednesdays after 4 p.m.

Chicago Museums with Free Admission Year-Round

In addition to select Chicago free museum days, several institutions offer complimentary admission to all visitors throughout the year. These museums provide excellent free things to do in Chicago for residents and tourists alike.

  • National Museum of Mexican Art
  • National Public Housing Museum
  • Climate Action Museum
  • National Museum of Puerto Rican Arts & Culture
  • Museum of Contemporary Photography
  • Smart Museum of Art
  • Hyde Park Art Center
  • Jane Addams Hull-House Museum
  • Mindworks: The Science of Thinking
  • National Veterans Art Museum
  • Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago Money Museum

These institutions offer diverse cultural, educational, and art-focused experiences and remain among the most accessible attractions in the city.

Frequently Asked Questions About Chicago Free Museum Days

What are Chicago free museum days?

Chicago free museum days are select dates when participating museums offer complimentary admission to Illinois residents with proof of residency.

Which are the best Chicago museums offering free admission?

The Field Museum, Shedd Aquarium, Adler Planetarium, Art Institute of Chicago, and Griffin Museum of Science and Industry are among the best Chicago museums offering free admission in 2026.

Are free museum days family-friendly?

Yes. Many participating museums feature interactive exhibits and educational programming, making them excellent family-friendly things to do in Chicago.

Do I need reservations for free museum days?

Some museums require advance online reservations even for free admission. Visitors should check each museum’s official website before visiting.

What are other free things to do in Chicago near Museum Campus?

In addition to museums, visitors can explore lakefront parks, walking trails, skyline viewpoints, and scenic boat rides.
