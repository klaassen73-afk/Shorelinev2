Skyline Lake Tour

Take in the stunning sights of Chicago’s skyline during a scenic boat tour on Lake Michigan. It’s a view of the city like you’ve never seen it before! Our Skyline Lake Tour is a popular Chicago skyline boat tour that departs daily from Navy Pier spring through autumn, weather permitting.

Enjoy 45 minutes of skyline views from the open water. This relaxed Lake Michigan boat tour is a great option for Chicago locals and visitors looking for an easy, memorable way to experience the city and one of the most accessible things to do in Chicago.

Cruises departing prior to 5:00 PM feature recorded narration highlighting the city’s skyline. Evening cruises will feature music and the same great views.

Tour highlights

✓ Cruises departing prior to 5:00 PM feature recorded narration of the city’s skyline. In the month of December, our 5:00, 6:00, and 7:00 PM tours will feature holiday music and a complimentary hot chocolate.
✓ View iconic landmarks including the Willis Tower, 875 N. Michigan (the former John Hancock Building), Aqua Tower, and The St. Regis Chicago (Vista Tower)
✓ Unique skyline views only visible from Lake Michigan
✓ Learn about Chicago’s buildings and how the city developed its famous skyline
✓ 45-minute boat cruise with recorded narration presented in two sections
